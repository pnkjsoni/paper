    Descriptive 2025 · Paper II · Part A · Q1 · 5 marks

    The Patents (Amendment) Rules, 2024 relaxed the burden of filing the information and undertaking regarding corresponding foreign applications, to improve ease of doing business. Discuss the amendments, citing the section, rules and forms.

    Points to cover (from the 2024 Rules)

    • Provisions: s. 8, Rule 12 and Form 3.
    • Rule 12(2): The period for updating changed from “six months from filing” to three months from issue of the first statement of objections (Rules 24B(3) or 24C(8)).
    • Rule 12(3): The Controller may use accessible databases to check foreign filings, so the applicant is not left alone to report them.
    • Rule 12(4): Under s. 8(2), the Controller may direct a fresh Form 3 within two months, for reasons recorded in writing.
    • Rule 12(5): Delay in filing Form 3 can be condoned, or time extended, by up to three months on a Form 4 request.
    • Form 3 itself: There is no fee, and the new format is simpler. The undertaking to keep the Controller informed runs only up to the date of grant.
    Amendment note: The 2023 key for Q1 reflects the old timelines. After the 2024 Rules, the update requirement is tied to the first statement of objections. Revise to the new rule, but if a question uses an old fact pattern, use the old answer.
